FEATURED · INDUSTRIAL ML · APS360

Bearing Fault Anomaly Detection with Autoencoders

Applied unsupervised learning to accelerometer measurements from industrial bearings. Converted raw signals to log-STFT spectrograms, trained a PyTorch convolutional autoencoder on normal data, and compared four classical anomaly-detection baselines. Designed source-file-level splits and normal-only threshold calibration to make the evaluation defensible.
